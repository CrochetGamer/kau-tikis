TIRE PLANTER

TIRE PLANTER DISCRIPTION: The Tire Planter is a fun and bright way to repurpose what most people toss on the side of the road: tires. Here in Hawaii, people throw used tires on the side of the road without a care - and it's a very rude and annoying thing to do. We here at Ka'u Tikis take pride in cleaning up the neighborhood - we pick up these tires and turn them into beautiful garden art. We clean them, change them into the gorgeous bulb you see, then we prime and paint them with several coats for longlasting durability in your garden. Order one of these bright statement pieces today.
