How to Order a Custom Fish

IF YOU'VE CAUGHT A FISH AND WANT TO MAKE IT INTO A METAL WALL ART, YOU'VE COME TO THE RIGHT PLACE!

WE WILL NEED DETAILED PICTURES OF YOUR FISH. THIS INCLUDES THE FINS, FACE, SIDE PROFILE, AND TAIL.

(THE PICTURES TO BE IN GOOD LIGHTING SO WE CAN GET THE COLORATION AND SCALE SHADING CORRECT.)

WE WILL ALSO NEED YOUR FISH DIMENSIONS:

- LENGTH FROM TIP OF NOSE TO TIP OF TAIL.
- WIDTH FROM TIP OF TOP, HIGHEST BACK FIN TO TIP OF BOTTOM, LOWEST STOMACH FIN.
