ULUA FISH 21 INCH METAL WALLHANG

ULUA DISCRIPTION: This Ulua Fish Metal Wallhanging measures 21 inches from head to tail tip and 10.5 inches wide from soft dorsal fin to the anal fin. This art piece is designed with the fishermen of Hawaii in mind. The thrill of catching huge ulua - also called giant trevally - is such a rush that the fishermen climb out onto precarious, dangerous cliffs in order to cast their lines into the deep. The ocean crashes in huge waves against the cliffside, and these gigantic fish swim through the waves, speeding by to catch their own dinner. This wallhanging has 2 holes available for the customer to place 2 screws for mounting, one located on the soft dorsal fin (largest top fin) and one located on the pelvic fin (smallest bottom fin).
